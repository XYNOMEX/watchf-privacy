Privacy Policy

XYNOMEX watch faces · Last updated 13 August 2026

Applies to all Wear OS watch faces published by XYNOMEX, including any released in future.

These watch faces collect nothing, store nothing, and send nothing anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchase. They request no permissions at all — including no internet permission — so they cannot transmit data even in principle.

What data is collected

None. No personal information and no device information is collected, stored or shared.

About the information shown on the face

Some faces can display heart rate, step count, battery level or the date. That information is drawn on screen by the Wear OS complication system, which is part of your watch's operating system.

  • Values are supplied by the system purely to be rendered, and appear only on your own watch.
  • They are never written to storage, never logged, and never transmitted.
  • Nothing leaves your device.

You choose which data source fills each slot, and you can remove any of them at any time: press and hold the watch face, then tap Customize.

Permissions

These apps declare no Android permissions whatsoever, including no internet permission. They are built with Google's Watch Face Format, a declarative format that contains no executable code (android:hasCode="false"), and the published packages contain only images and layout definitions.

Children

These apps contain no ads, no purchases and no data collection. Nothing in them is directed at children specifically, and they are safe for any age to use.
